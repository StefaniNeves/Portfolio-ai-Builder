# Measuring business impact (do not invent results)

This repository includes sample data only. It does not claim real customer outcomes or time
savings. To measure impact in a pilot:

1. Select one recurring reporting task and document the manual baseline.
2. Time at least five comparable manual runs.
3. Time at least five runs using the tool, including corrections and review.
4. Compare median completion time, error count, and percentage of records processed.
5. Record sample size, dates, assumptions, and limitations.

Suggested calculation:

`time saved (%) = ((manual median - assisted median) / manual median) * 100`

Publish the measured values only after running the experiment. Separate measured results from
estimates and never use synthetic sample data as evidence of customer impact.
