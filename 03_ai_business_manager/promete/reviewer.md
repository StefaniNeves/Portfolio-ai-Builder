# Reviewer — evaluation prompt

Review the business analysis against the supplied metrics.

Check: (1) factual consistency, (2) unsupported claims, (3) whether each recommendation
is actionable, (4) whether facts and hypotheses are separated, and (5) whether limitations
are acknowledged. Do not add new business facts. Return PASS or NEEDS_REVISION followed
by concise findings. A human remains responsible for approving recommendations.
